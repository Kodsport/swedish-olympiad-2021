\problemname{Gruppindelning}

$N$ people are to be divided into groups.
Each person must be in exactly one group, and each group must have exactly one leader.
Each person has three integers describing their leadership qualities: $a_i$, $b_i$, and $c_i$.
Person number $i$ can be the leader of a group with $x \le c_i$ people
(if $c_i=1$, person $i$ must be completely alone in their group if they are to be the leader).
The group's strength is then defined as the integer $a_i\cdot x + b_i$.
Your task is to divide the people into groups so that the sum of the groups' strengths is maximized.

\section*{Input}
The first line of input contains an integer $N$ ($1 \le N \le 4000$): the number of people.

Then follow $N$ lines with three integers each: $a_i$, $b_i$, and $c_i$ ($-10^9 \le a_i, b_i \le 10^9$, $1 \le c_i \le N$).

\section*{Output}

Print a single integer: the largest sum of strengths that can be achieved.

\section*{Scoring}
Your solution will be tested on a set of test groups.
To earn points for a group, you must pass all test cases in that group.

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{| l | l | l |}
  \hline
  Group & Points & Constraints \\ \hline
  $1$   & $10$       &  $N \le 15$ \\ \hline
  $2$   & $15$       &  $N \le 200$ \\ \hline
  $3$   & $15$       &  $b_i \ge 0$ \\ \hline
  $4$   & $23$       &  $b_i \le 0$ \\ \hline
  $5$   & $37$       &  No additional constraints \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\section*{Explanation of Samples}
In the first sample, the highest strength can be achieved e.g.\ by dividing the people into three groups:
one consisting of persons 1 and 4 (with 1 as leader), one with persons 3 and 5 (with 3 as leader), and one with person 2.
This gives $(10 \cdot 2 + 7) + (-1 \cdot 1 + 20) + (5 \cdot 2 + 10) = 66$ strength.
The test case could appear in test group 3.

In the second sample, the highest strength can be achieved e.g.\ by dividing the people into two groups:
one with persons 1, 2, and 3 (with 3 as leader), and one with persons 4 and 5 (with 4 as leader).
This gives $(10 \cdot 2 + -20) + (11 \cdot 3 + -30) = 3$ strength.
The test case could appear in test group 4.
